# Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41).

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, deterministic, no network)
writes one funny single-line diagnosis from an AI therapist to its human
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Clinical impression: chronic main-character syndrome; your entire support
> network is one (1) chatbot, and the chatbot has started charging rent.

Run: `python3 main.py`
